Robotics Competitions

The Green MacHHHHine competed in two different robotics competition leagues.  Originally competing in the FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC), we decided after two years that we wanted to compete in the VEX Robotics Competition instead for a number of reasons.

Overview

These two leagues are structured very similarly, and are mostly geared towards high school students (although both FIRST and VEX have offshoot leagues for elementary schools, middle schools, and colleges).  Every year, both leagues issue a challenge in the form of a game that teams have to build a robot to play.  Teams build their robot and bring them to local tournaments where they compete with each other to win the tournament, awards, and an invitation to the World Championships.

Check It Out!

If you or someone you know is interested (or even potentially interested) in robotics, we highly recommend going to check out a local competition! Almost all FIRST and VEX competitions are free to spectate. Once you've watched a few matches at the main field, head back to the "pits" and chat with some actual team members!  Teams are generally more than happy to discuss their robots' designs and capabilities. Click these links to find some local competitions in your area:  FRC Competitions, and VEX Competitions (be sure to filter for the "VEX Robotics Competition" under "Programs").

Get Involved

If you've been convinced and want to join a robotics team, check with your school to see if they have one.  If not, you can found your own team, or you can check with other schools in your area to see if they have a team you can join.

If you are out of school but want to be involved and have experience to share, many teams are looking for adult mentors to help guide them (but not build their robots for them).  Check with local teams in your area to see if they would like some help – we appreciate our adult mentors!
