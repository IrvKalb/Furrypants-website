2010-2011 Season

Back to History Page

After a year in the FIRST Robotics Competition, we transitioned to the VEX Robotics Competition in 2010.  In VEX, teams spend all year long designing, building, and testing their robots, and can make changes to their robots between competitions.  The robots are also much smaller – they typically weigh around 15 pounds and are about 18 inches tall.  Additionally, our typical yearly budget in the FIRST Robotics Competition was about $20,000 to $30,000, while a year in VEX required only $2,000 to $3,000.  For more infomration on VEX, visit the VEX website linked in the sidebar.


Here is an album of photos from this season


We went to three regional competitions this season, playing the game "Round Up". (Here is a video that explains the game.)   The first competition we attended was in Walnut Creek, California, the second one was in Tracy, California, and the third one was in San Jose, California.


Tracy Competition

Our first tournament of the season, on October 30th, 2010, took place in Tracy, California, and was our first competition ever as a VEX Robotics team.  Without knowing quite what to expect, we seeded 7th out of 25 teams, but our alliance was knocked out in the quarterfinals.

San Ramon Competition

Hungry for a victory, we went to the tournament in San Ramon, California. After going undefeated in the qualification rounds, we captained the 3rd seeded alliance to a tournament victory over 29 other teams!  That's right – we won the second VEX tournament we ever competed in!

San Jose Competition

At our third competition, in San Jose, California, we went undefeated in the qualification rounds again. We seeded third and captained our alliance, but we lost in the final match. However, we won the tournament's "Excellence" award over 50 other teams. This is the most prestigious award given out at the tournament. Here is the official description of the award:

"This is the highest award presented in the VEX Robotics Competition. The recipient of this award is a team that exemplifies overall excellence in building a well-rounded VEX program. This team excels in many areas and is a shining example of dedication, devotion, hard work, and team work. A strong contender in numerous award categories, this team deserves to be recognized for their accomplishments building a robot, and in building a team committed to quality in everything they do."

2011 World Championships

We attended the world championships in Florida (April 14th-16th) after receiving three invitations.  The world championship was a great success for our team!  We placed 6th in the world in the programming skills challenge when we scored 35 points.  Our record in the elimination matches was 7 wins, 2 losses, and we seeded 10th out of 104 teams in our Math division.  We allied with teams 1530 from Indiana and 2213A from Puerto Rico, and made it to the semi-finals in our division.  We went on to win the divisional Excellence award, which means that the judges ranked us as the overall top team in our division.

Out of the 3,600 VEX teams in the world, only four can claim that they are 2011 divisional Excellence award winners!
