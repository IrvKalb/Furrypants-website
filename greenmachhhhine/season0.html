Here is an album of pictures from this season.

The Green MacHHHHine team, team number 3129, was founded in the year 2009 as an FRC (FIRSTRobotics Competition) team.  Every year,FRC teams spend a six-week "build season" designing, building, and testing a robot to complete a challenge issued by the FIRST organization.  These robots are generally around six feet tall and weigh up to 120 pounds.  See the sidebar for a link to the FRC page on the FIRST website.

We were originally affiliated with the 4-H organization, which is where the four H's in "Green MacHHHHine" come from.  Founded after the 2009 FRC world championships, we decided to build a robot to compete in the off-season competition, CalGames, playing the game "Lunacy".  Here is the game animation for Lunacy, which explains the basics of the game.

 

Competing as team #9999 at CalGames (as we had not yet been assigned our official team number and were considered a "pre-rookie"), we won the "Highest Rookie Seed" award!

Off the field, we started a community outreach program, where we taught lego robotics to younger students. It was very successful; we had about 25 younger students at every meeting.  The team also demonstrated our robot and gave talks about our organization for large corporations, younger students, and a local HOPE facility.
