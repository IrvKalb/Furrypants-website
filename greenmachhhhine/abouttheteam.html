About the Team

 

The Green MacHHHHine, team 3129A, was a robotics team made up of high school students from many different schools.  Afer competing in the FIRST Robotics Competition for almost two years, we decided to switch over to the VEX Robotics Competition for its longer build season and more manageable size. Every year, the FIRST and VEX organizations released a challenge, and we would design, build, test, and drive a robot to complete the required tasks.  Every year, we competed at multiple local touraments and earned invitations to compete at the world championships- more details about our on-the-field successes can be found under the "History" tab on this website!
