Here is an album of photos from this season.

The VEX Robotics challenge this year was called "Toss Up", and you can see an animation explaining the game here. This season, we attended four competitions: two local tournaments, the California State Championships, and the World Championships.

San Ramon Competition

At our first tournament of the season, in San Ramon, we seeded 11th out of 61 teams, made it to the quarterfinals, and won the highest honor at the tournament: the "Excellence Award"! Here is the official description of the Excellence Award:

"This is the highest award presented in the VEX Robotics Competition. The recipient of this award is a team that exemplifies overall excellence in building a well-rounded VEX robotics program.  This team excels in many areas and is a shining example of dedication, devotion, hard work and teamwork.  As a strong contender in numerous award categories, this team deserves to be recognized for building a quality robot and a “team” committed to quality in everything that they do."

San Jose Competition

At our second competition of the season, in San Jose, we were chosen to compete on the third seeded alliance, and we made it all the way to the semifinals!  We also won the "Design Award" at the tournament, earning us our second invitation to compete at the California State Championships in March!

California State Championships 2014

In March, we attended the California State Championships in Santa Clara!  After seeding 4th in the qualification rounds with a record of 7 wins and 1 loss, we captained the third seeded alliance to the quarterfinals!  We received an invitation to attend the World Championships in April!


World Championships 2014

The 2014 VEX World Championships in Anaheim, California, was the last competition that the Green MacHHHHine competed in.  With a record of 6 wins and 4 losses in the qualifying rounds, we seeded 20th out of 86 teams in the Technology division but fell short of competing in the elimination rounds.  We did, however, compete in the online challenges, winning 1st place in the world (for the second time) in the Educational Video Award category, 3rd in the Website category, 1st in the Essay challenge, and we were a finalist in the Promotional Video Award category!  You can read our award-winning esssay, "VEX Connects", here.
