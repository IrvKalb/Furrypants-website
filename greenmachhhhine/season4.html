2012-2013 Season

Back to History Page

   

Here is an album of photos from this season.

This year, our team competed at three local tournaments, playing the game, "Sack Attack" (you can see an animation explaining the game here).  The first competition was in San Ramon, California, the second one was in San Jose, California, and the third was in Tracy, California.

San Ramon Competition

At the San Ramon tournament, we seeded 6th out of 40 teams and made it all the way to the finals!  Also, we won the Excellence Award at the tournament, earning us an invitation to compete at the 2013 World Championships!  Here is the official description of the Excellence Award:

"This is the highest award presented in the VEX Robotics Competition. The recipient of this award is a team that exemplifies overall excellence in building a well-rounded VEX robotics program.  This team excels in many areas and is a shining example of dedication, devotion, hard work and teamwork.  As a strong contender in numerous award categories, this team deserves to be recognized for building a quality robot and a “team” committed to quality in everything that they do."


San Jose Competition

At the San Jose tournament, we seeded 6th out of 54 teams and captained our alliance to the quarterfinals!

Tracy Competition

At the third tournament, we seeded 17th out of 66 teams and made it to the quarterfinals!

2013 World Championships

With that, we headed down to Anaheim, California, to compete with about 700 other top teams from around the world at the 2013 VEX Robotics World Championships.  With a record of 8-2 in the qualifying rounds, we seeded 7th out of 84 teams in the Science division, and captained the 6th seed alliance to the quarterfinals (allied with teams 2323Z and 1045A).  Also, we won the Excellence Award for our division!  This means that the judges ranked us as the overall top team in the Science Division!  We are very proud to have won the top honors!

Out of the tens of thousands of VEX teams in the world, only five can claim that they are 2013 divisional Excellence award winners! This was our second divisional Excellence award win after competing at only three World Championships!
