2009-2010 Season

Back to History Page

In the 2009-2010 season, our first official season as an FRC team, we built a robot to play the game "Breakaway".  Here is the game animation that explains the basic rules for Breakaway.

We entered one competition this season – the Silicon Valley Regional located in San Jose. At this tournament, we won both the "Highest Rookie Seed" and "Rookie All-Star" awards!  We were also awarded the custom "Coolest Rookie" award from team 701, the Robovikes.

   

Here is an album of pictures from this season.

Off the field, we had a community outreach program, where we taught lego robotics to younger students. It was very successful; we had about 25 younger students at every meeting.

We also demonstrated our robots and discussed comptetitive robotics with large corporations, younger students, and a local HOPE facility.
