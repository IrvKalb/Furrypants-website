Welcome to the Green MacHHHHine Website!

Green MacHHHHine Team #3129A was a high school robotics team from Mountain View, California that competed in the FIRST and VEX Robotics Competitions.

Mission and Philosophy

Our mission was to sustain an organization where students could learn about science and engineering from professionals in those fields.  Students worked in a team environment motivated by exciting competition against other teams.

Our team was made up of both students and adult mentors.  Our philosophy was that the role of mentors on our team was to teach the students and advise them.  The students on our team not only learned about robotics but were also the only ones that built the robots.

Since graduating high school, all of our student members have since attended college with the majority earning degrees in STEM-related fields.

On-Field Accomplishments

Perhaps our proudest accolade in competing was winning the divisional "Excellence Award" at two of the four VEX Robotics World Championships that we attended.  Each year, the divisional Excellence Award is given out to only four teams in the world, and consideration for the award encompasses on-field performance as well as the impact that the team has made off the field. Here is the official description of the Excellence Award:

"This is the highest award presented in the VEX Robotics Competition. The recipient of this award is a team that exemplifies overall excellence in building a well-rounded VEX robotics program.  This team excels in many areas and is a shining example of dedication, devotion, hard work and teamwork.  As a strong contender in numerous award categories, this team deserves to be recognized for building a quality robot and a “team” committed to quality in everything that they do."
